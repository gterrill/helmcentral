# What Mate can look up

[Mate](../features/assistant.md) answers most questions from the boat's own
live instrument data. For anything else it uses the lookups below, each tied
to a provider you configure elsewhere in Helmcentral. This page is the detail
behind the summary on Mate's own page. For each lookup it says what it draws
on, what has to be configured for it to work, and what Mate says when it can't
be done.

| Lookup | What it reads | Comes from | Needs configured | If it can't run |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Live vessel context | Position, heading, speed, apparent wind, current place name, marine warning in force | The dashboard's own live feed, exactly as shown right now | Nothing extra, always included | A missing reading reads as "unknown", never a guessed number |
| Place lookup | Resolves a named place to a position, with its straight-line distance and bearing from the boat for comparison | The configured place-names plugin, searched by exact name within about 200 nautical miles of the boat | A place-names plugin (**Settings → Plugins → Place names**, OpenStreetMap by default) | Says the name didn't resolve |
| Marina details | What OpenStreetMap records about the marina charted at a position: berths, fee, power, contact details, fuel, travel lift, boatyard, ramp, water, pump-out, toilets, showers, pontoon arm names, entrance beacons and buoys, and named shops and services within about 1 km. It also compares any mapped maximum draught and length with the boat's draft and length overall. Shown as a marina card with a map of the outline, pontoons and facilities, and the date the map data was last updated. It also names up to three documents you've tagged with the marina's name, which Mate then reads for berth numbers and the layout, and the card links to them | The place-names plugin's marina lookup, which makes two requests to the Overpass server: one to find the marina within 500 m of the position, one for what is mapped beside it. The documents come from your library | The `osm-overpass` plugin as the place-names plugin, and an internet connection. The boat's draft and length overall for the limit check. The document library, which every install has | Says no marina is charted within 500 m, or that the plugin has no marina lookup, or the map server's own error. A limit that isn't mapped is reported as not charted, and a boat figure that isn't set as not set, never assumed. What isn't listed is unknown, not absent |
| Anchorage details | For a stay at a position, from your arrival to your departure. Without a leave time the stay ends at the first 08:00 local time at least six hours after arrival, and a stay can be up to seven days. It reads the shelter from each wind direction. It reads wind, gusts, direction and waves every three hours from the hour nearest arrival, with the shelter grade of each row and each day. It reads the tide height at arrival, each high and low water through the stay, and their range. It also reads what OpenStreetMap records within 1 nm. That is restricted areas and marine protected areas, public moorings with their vessel size limit and class where mapped, anchorage areas, seabed areas and the bay. Shown as an anchorage card with a shelter ring, the forecast and tide tables, the features, and a map folded under **Map**. It gives no depth and no holding | Shelter from the [Offline coastline](../features/offline-coastline.md) held on the boat. The forecast from the weather and wave providers above, and the tide from the tide station nearest the position. The map features from the place-names plugin's anchorage lookup, which makes one request to the Overpass server | The Offline coastline turned on and downloaded for the area. A weather provider, optionally a wave provider, and a tide provider for those parts. The `osm-overpass` plugin as the place-names plugin, in a version that has the anchorage lookup, and an internet connection for the map features | Shelter is required: if the coastline is off, still downloading or doesn't cover the position, the whole lookup fails and Mate says why. The forecast, the tide and the map features are each checked on their own: one that fails shows its error and nothing else, and Mate says it wasn't checked. Nothing is filled in. A tide height at a time outside the predicted tides is left out. What isn't listed is unknown, not absent. A moorings list with no size or class is shown as details not tagged |
| Forecast for a place | Wind and wave forecast for a named place, not just where you are now | The same providers the Forecast panel uses | A forecast provider configured | Names the provider that failed |
| Tide for a place | Tide predictions for a named place | The same tide provider and station catalogue the dashboard uses | A tide provider and station | Says no tide provider is configured |
| Passage wind and sea angle | True wind and sea angle against a planned course | Worked out from the forecast lookup above, not estimated by a language model | A course to compare against | - |
| Fair-tide departure | The best three departure times, and the one to avoid, for a passage, with the share of the run that has the stream with you and the flood and ebb spells it crosses | The tide times of the nearest tide station, the passage course and hours, and the direction the flood stream sets, which Mate takes from your standing notes first and otherwise from its own general knowledge and says which | A tide provider and station. Putting the local flood direction, and how long the stream turns after high and low water if you know it, in your standing notes makes Mate use those | Says what is missing: no tide provider or station, tide times that do not reach the passage, or a flood direction it does not know. It reports stream phase only, not stream rate, and says the direction is to be checked against the tidal stream atlas and cruising guide |
| Weather window | Go, marginal, no-go or no data for each leg of a trip on each candidate departure day, with the times and the figure that decided each, shown as a card under the answer | The forecast providers above, sampled along each leg for the hours it would be under way, sunset times for the leg's end, and, for legs with a known flood direction, the tide station's high and low water | Your [passage limits](../how-to/set-passage-limits.md), with a no-go wind limit for every sector a leg passes through (marginal is 70% of no-go, and gusts are judged against the no-go wind plus 10 kn), and a forecast provider. A wave provider and a no-go wave height add the wave check, and a tide provider and a wind-against-tide reduction add that one | Takes passage hours or a speed for each leg (the hours win if both are given, and Mate is told the speed was ignored). Says the limits are not set, or names the sector without one. A leg past the forecast is no data. A check it could not make (no gust forecast, no wave forecast, no tide station, no flood direction, sunset unknown) is listed as not checked, never passed |
| Distance by water | How far it is between two positions by the shortest water, round islands and headlands. It gives the straight line for comparison, the course of the longest leg, each leg and the bends. A very long route may lose its waypoints but keeps its distance | The [offline coastline](../features/offline-coastline.md), searched for a route that keeps about 90 m off the charted shore | The offline coastline on, with the squares round both ends downloaded | Says it can't give a distance and why. The coastline is off, a square is missing (it names it), a position is on land, no water joins the two, or the run is over 300 nm. It never gives the straight line instead |
| Passage time and fuel | Estimated time and fuel burn for a run to a destination, over the distance by water above, at a speed | This vessel's own logged speed-over-ground and fuel-rate history, not a manufacturer's curve, and the same route over water | InfluxDB, and the offline coastline as above | Mate reasons about the passage in general terms instead. If an engine's fuel rate or RPM was not logged in the window, it names the engine, and says to check its source in **Settings → SignalK** when that source has never reported RPM. "No underway history" means the boat simply did not move in the window |
| Nearby vessels | Other boats currently in range (range, bearing, speed, and how long each has been nearby), or the last time a named boat was seen if it has since moved on | The same AIS targets the Nearby Vessels tile shows, plus the record of past encounters | Nothing extra. A tighter "how long has it been sitting there" figure needs the boat's own data logger set up to record other vessels' tracks, which most boats leave off | Says no vessel by that name is in range or on record |
| Boat status | One snapshot: house bank charge, voltage and current, solar watts, DC load, each tank's level and alarm levels, depth, true wind and gust, pressure and its three hour change, engines, anchor, active and ignored alarms, the sensor check counts and silent sensors, and the planned stay with its distance from the boat. Odd things below an alarm are listed apart as observations. Today that is the house bank draining faster overnight than usual: last night at least 1.5 times the median of at least 3 earlier nights, and at least 0.25 percentage points an hour more | The same readings the tiles show, found through the roles and equipment you set up. Figures are in your units. Pressure is in hPa | The house bank role, tanks and engines on the Equipment page, and for the overnight check InfluxDB | Leaves a reading out and gives the reason: no role set, or no fresh reading (over 2 minutes old, or 5 for pressure and position). It never estimates, and never uses an older value. The overnight check says why it could not run |
| Active alarms | The alarms active right now, worst first: what each says, when it was raised, whether it is acknowledged or silenced, the rule behind it, and for a sensor alarm (impossible reading, frozen, silent source) the actual reading that tripped it, as it was when the alarm was raised and as it is now | The same alarm list the alarm card and banner show | Nothing extra, always available | Says nothing is alarming when the list is empty, and quotes times in boat local time. Mate checks the reading the alarm names, not one it guesses from a device name on the card |
| Alarm context | One alarm explained: the alarm as its card shows it, the rule behind it, the live reading and how old it is, the reading's lowest, average and highest in the half hour either side of the alarm, how many times it was raised in the last 7 days, and the other alarms raised or cleared within 15 minutes | The alarm list and the alarm log, the rule list, the live reading, and InfluxDB for the history. Works on a live alarm or one that has cleared. Used when you tap **Ask Mate** on an alarm | InfluxDB for the history | Leaves out what it can't give, with the reason: no rule for an alarm raised elsewhere on the network, no reading behind a collision or radar alarm, no history without InfluxDB. It never lists related readings, because Helmcentral keeps no grouping of them |
| Is a reading live right now | Whether the boat's instrument connection is up, which feeds are updating and which have gone quiet, and the current value and age of any reading you name | The same live feed every tile reads from, checked directly rather than through one tile's own display | Nothing extra, always available | Says the connection is down, or that nothing matching what you named is currently reporting |
| Watch a reading | Up to six live readings, once a second, for 1 to 30 minutes, then a report: range, average, steadiness, spikes and dips with their times, stretches with no reading, and for two readings the difference between them second by second | The same live feed every tile reads from. The watch is also kept on the [Watches](../features/watches.md) page | Nothing extra. Mate must be switched on when the watch ends for the report to be explained | Names the reading that isn't live, isn't a number or hasn't updated in the last 10 seconds, and doesn't start. Refuses a second watch in the same conversation, or a fourth across Helmcentral. **Stop**, or deleting the conversation, cancels the watch with no report. A restart doesn't: the watch is saved and carries on |
| When a reading stopped | The last recorded time and value for a reading, and what it was doing (steady, dropping out, frozen on one value) before then | Your boat's own long-term telemetry history | InfluxDB | Says no history log is set up, or that nothing recorded matches what you asked about |
| Chart of logged readings | Up to four logged readings over a date range, as stacked panels over time (one unit to a panel, directions in degrees as dots, a min to max band behind each line) or one against another, optionally across up to three date ranges you name, drawn as a card whose dates you can change | Your boat's own long-term telemetry history, converted into your units using the unit the boat reports for each reading | InfluxDB. Readings named by their job (house bank, alternators, engines) need those roles chosen in **Settings → SignalK → Roles** | Names the role that is not chosen, the reading with nothing logged in a range, or the devices to choose between when two report the same reading. A reading whose unit the boat does not report is drawn unconverted and says so |
| Wind rose | Where the true wind came from over a date range, counted into 8, 16 or 32 compass sectors and speed bands in knots, with calm and variable wind counted apart, drawn as a card | Your boat's own long-term telemetry history of true wind direction and speed | InfluxDB, with the boat reporting true wind direction in radians and speed in metres a second | Says when the direction or speed was never logged in the range, or which devices to choose between when two logged it. It never averages devices |
| Recent passages | The periods the boat was underway over the last two weeks (or up to 60 days when asked), most recent first, with start, finish, duration, approximate distance and speed. Mate uses them to look up what a reading did on each one, such as when the alternators stopped charging | Your boat's own logged speed over ground. Underway means 3 knots or more. A slow-down shorter than 30 minutes stays part of the same passage, and runs under 15 minutes are ignored | InfluxDB | Says no history log is set up, or that no speed was logged in the period |
| What you're looking at | Which panel, dashboard page or document the question was asked from | The screen behind the Mate sheet when you ask, sent with every question asked there. The full Mate panel sends none | Nothing extra | - |
| Helmcentral's own features | How a feature works, quoting the actual page | The in-app help, the same pages the header's **?** opens | Nothing extra | - |
| Equipment and maintenance | Items in the equipment registry with their hour-meter reading and their jobs from the item's profile, marking any changed for that item and naming a profile that's missing, every maintenance rule with its status today, intervals, last-done baseline and procedure note, and the service log with dates, hours, who, cost and parts | Inventory → Equipment and Maintenance, exactly as those pages show them, with status worked out against today's date at the helm | Nothing extra, see [Maintenance](../features/maintenance.md) | Says the maintenance data is unavailable. An item with no reading is reported as hours unknown, never as zero |
| Proposed schedule changes | A card of changes to the maintenance schedule: add a rule, change a rule's description or intervals, set what was last done, log a job as done or acknowledge a rule. A change to a job from an item's profile, including marking it not applicable, applies to that item only. Every hours figure is a meter reading, what the gauge shows | Written by Mate after it has read the schedule, and made only when you tap **Apply**, all changes together or none | Write access to apply. A read-only session sees the card with no buttons | Says which field is wrong and fixes it before showing you a card. **Apply** refuses, and says why, if a rule changed after Mate wrote the card. Mate cannot delete rules or log entries, edit log entries, or add photos, parts, meter replacements or procedure notes |
| Alarm rules | Every alarm the boat watches: its name, the reading it watches, the level it fires and clears at in your units, how serious it is, how long the reading has to hold, whether it's switched on, and whether it's your own, built in, or comes from a tank's levels or a gauge zone | The Alarms drawer's rules list, plus the tank and gauge alarms it shows as read-only | Nothing extra, see [Alarms](../features/alarms.md) | Says the alarm rules aren't available |
| Proposed alarms | A card that adds an alarm, changes one (its levels, how serious it is, how long it must hold, on or off) or removes one. Levels are in your units | Written by Mate after it has read the existing alarms, and made only when you tap **Apply** | Write access to apply. A read-only session sees the card with no buttons | Says which field is wrong and fixes it before showing you a card. **Apply** refuses, and says why, if the alarm changed after Mate wrote the card. Mate can't change a tank or gauge alarm or an alarm's reading once it's made, and it won't say an alarm is set until you've applied the card |
| Watches | The scheduled watches the boat has: name, schedule and what it means in words, the report sections, the thresholds, what to look out for, whether it's on, when it last ran and how many findings that run had | The watches you've set up on the Watches page | Nothing extra, see [Watches](../features/watches.md) | Says the watches aren't available |
| Watch runs | A watch's latest runs, newest first, or one run: the report by section, the findings the rules reached, Mate's review, whether it was skipped and why, and what the review cost. Used when you tap **Ask Mate about this run** | The saved history of the Watches page | Nothing extra | Says no such watch or run exists, and to list the watches for the ids |
| Run a watch now | Runs a scheduled watch straight away and gives its run. Saved like any other run | The same report and review a scheduled run makes | Mate switched on for the review. A boat position at some point, for the boat's time | Says the boat's time isn't known, or that the watches have stopped and why. If Mate isn't ready, the run is saved with the report and the reason there's no review |
| Proposed watches | A card that adds a watch, changes one (its schedule, sections, thresholds, what to look out for, on or off) or removes one. The schedule is shown in words | Written by Mate after it has read the existing watches, and made only when you tap **Apply** | Write access to apply. A read-only session sees the card with no buttons | Says which field is wrong and fixes it before showing you a card. **Apply** refuses, and says why, if the watch changed after Mate wrote the card. A schedule that runs more often than every 15 minutes is refused |
| Saved places | The positions you gave Mate for named spots, found by name with distance and bearing from the boat. Mate looks here before it asks the map, and says when it's using one | The places saved under **Settings → Saved places** | Nothing extra | A name that isn't saved goes on to the map lookup |
| Proposed saved places | A card that saves a named position you gave Mate, or moves one it already has, with the old and new position shown in degrees and minutes | Written by Mate when you give it a position for a spot it didn't know, and made only when you tap **Apply** | Write access to apply. A read-only session sees the card with no buttons | Says which field is wrong and fixes it before showing you a card. A latitude or longitude out of range is refused |
| Proposed document tags | A card that adds a tag to a document or takes one off, shown as the tags before and after. Mate uses it to link a marina's map to the marina when you ask, by adding a tag that is the marina's name. Only your own tags can change. The title, notes, folder and file can't, and Mate can't add or delete a document | Written by Mate after you ask, and made only when you tap **Apply**. Never because of something it read on the web | Write access to apply. A read-only session sees the card with no buttons | Says which field is wrong and fixes it before showing you a card. A blank tag is refused. **Apply** refuses, and says why, if the document's tags changed after Mate wrote the card |
| Vessel details | The owner's name, phone and email, insurer and policy number, home marina and berth, storm delegate, length overall and beam, and the hull details, each marked as stored or as coming from the live instruments, plus the boat's name and draft from the instruments | **Settings → Vessel**, and the live feed when connected | Nothing extra. Details left blank stay blank | Lists what is not on record, so Mate asks you instead of filling a guess. Says so if the live feed cannot be read, and flags a length that differs between the two |
| Reading a PDF form | For a fillable form, each field's name, kind, choices and current value. For a flat form, each page's lines of text and tick boxes with the words beside them, with their positions | The PDF you attached or filed in Documents | Nothing extra | Says no tick boxes were found, and asks you what to tick. Cannot read a scan, which has no text |
| Filling in a PDF form | A copy of the form with the answers written in, shown as a card to open, download, save to Documents or dismiss. Signatures are never filled | Written by Mate from what you and the vessel details gave it, and put in Documents only when you press **Save to Documents** | Nothing extra to fill. Write access to save or dismiss | Names the field, line or box it could not find, or the value that would not fit, and makes nothing. A signature line is refused |
| Earlier conversations | Past conversations that mention a piece of equipment or a topic, with the matching lines and the date, or a whole earlier conversation read through | Your saved Mate conversations, the same ones the conversation list shows. The conversation you are in is left out | Nothing extra | Says nothing matches. A deleted conversation cannot be found |
| The document library | Manuals, receipts, notes, photos already in the library | An attached file, or a search across the whole library | See [Documents](../features/documents.md) | - |

Place search uses the same plugin as the place name on the position tile. When
that plugin is OpenStreetMap, it uses the same Overpass server setting too. It's
a separate choice from the plugin the Nearby tile uses, even though both default
to the same one. If your boat's connection can't reach the default Overpass
server, see [Configuration → Overpass](configuration.md#overpass) for how to
point it at a mirror.

If a lookup fails three times while Mate is answering a single question, Mate
doesn't try it again for the rest of that answer. It says the lookup was
unavailable and answers with what it has.
