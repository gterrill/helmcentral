# Film a passage replay

You can make the replay camera follow the boat, circle her at the anchorage or
slow down for the stretch worth watching. You can also pin notes and photos to
the places along the way, so that a passage plays like a short film. See
[Passages](../features/passages.md) for what shots and signs are.

Open **Passages**, then the passage, and wait for the map.

## Add a shot

1. Drag the replay slider to where the shot should begin.
2. Press **Camera** on the replay bar to open the shot editor.
3. Press **Add shot here**.

A mark appears on the slider. The new shot is a **Follow** shot at the zoom and
tilt the map has now, so set the view you like first and the shot starts there.
A shot lasts until the next one, or until the end of the replay.

## Change a shot

Pick the mark on the slider. The replay jumps there and the shot's settings
open.

1. Choose **Overview**, **Follow** or **Orbit**.
2. Set **Zoom** and **Pitch**. A higher pitch looks more toward the horizon.
3. For Follow, set **Angle**: 0 looks from astern, 90 from abeam, and a negative
   number from the other side.
4. For Orbit, set **Orbit °/s**, which is the degrees she turns each second you
   watch. A negative number circles the other way.
5. Press Enter, or move to the next field, to save a number.

To copy a view you've set by hand, drag and tilt the map the way you want it,
then press **Use current view**. It copies the zoom and pitch, and for Follow it
also copies the angle you're looking from.

## Slow a stretch down

Under **Pace** choose **1×**, **½×** or **¼×**. At ½× the passage runs at half
speed for as long as that shot lasts, so the whole replay takes longer to watch.
The slider still counts in passage time. The length list (**60 s**, **30 s**,
**15 s** or **8 s**) still sets the length of the replay at full pace.

## Take the camera yourself

If you drag, zoom, pinch or tilt the map while the replay plays, the replay
leaves the camera with you. A simple tap doesn't count. The replay takes the
camera back when the next shot starts, or when you press Play or move the
slider.

## Place the overview map

1. Turn on the **Overview map** switch on the replay bar.
2. Press the slider icon beside it, **Place overview map**.
3. Under **Corner**, choose **Top left**, **Top right**, **Bottom left** or
   **Bottom right**.
4. Under **Zoom**, press **Wider** to show more of the coast or **Closer** to
   show less. The number between them is how many steps you are from the
   automatic zoom, from -3 to +3, and 0 is the automatic zoom.

The choice is saved with the passage. A blog embed or image made from the
passage afterwards uses the same corner and zoom. A cruise or a selection of
passages remembers its own choice, separate from its first passage's. If you
trim a passage, or replay a different set, it starts again from the defaults.

## Remove a shot

Pick its mark and press **Delete shot**.

## Add a sign

A sign is a short note, with a photo if you like, pinned to a moment of the
passage. It appears on the map as the boat goes by.

1. Drag the replay slider to the moment.
2. Press **Signs** on the replay bar to open the sign editor.
3. Press **Add sign here**.
4. Type a **Title** and, if you want, a **Note**. Click outside the field to
   save it.

A diamond appears on the slider. Pick it to jump back and edit the sign.

Choose when it shows:

- **While passing** (the default) shows it just before the boat reaches the
  spot and keeps it up for **Seconds shown**, from 1 to 30.
- **After passing** shows it from the spot to the end of the replay.
- **Always** shows it for the whole replay.

Only two signs show at once. When a third is due, the one that has been up
longest steps aside. A sign set to **Always** stays, so two of them leave no
room for the rest.

## Add a photo to a sign

1. Open the sign editor with **Signs**.
2. To make a new sign from a photo, press **Add photo sign**. Then choose
   **Upload photo** to pick a picture from your device, or **From Documents** to
   use one already in the library.
3. To put a photo on a sign you already have, pick the sign, press **Photo**,
   then choose **Upload** or **From Documents**.
4. To take it off, pick the sign, press **Photo**, then **Remove**.

When you make a sign from a photo, it goes to the moment the photo was taken if
that moment is inside the replay, and the replay jumps there. The editor tells
you the time it used. If the photo has no time zone, the time is read in the
boat's time zone, which is the one shown beside the replay time. If the photo
has no time, or it was taken outside the replay, the sign goes where the slider
is and the editor says so.

## Move a sign to another moment

1. Open the sign editor with **Signs** and pick the sign.
2. To put it where the slider is, drag the slider there and press **Move sign
   here**.
3. To shift it a little, press **−1 min**, **−10 s**, **+10 s** or **+1 min**.
   The replay jumps to the new moment each time, and quick presses add up.
4. For a sign with a photo, press **Photo**, then **Back to photo time** to put
   it where the photo was taken.

A sign can only be moved to a moment the replay includes. If you try anywhere else,
the editor says so and the sign stays where it was.

Uploaded photos are kept in a Documents folder called **Passage photos**. If you
delete a photo there, the sign keeps its title and note and shows no picture.

## Look at a sign's photo

Before you press Play, every sign is a small mark on the track. Tap one to open
its card. While the replay is paused, tap a card to open its photo full size.
Press Escape to close it. While the replay plays, a tap does nothing, so you
don't lose the picture by accident.

## Delete a sign

Pick its diamond on the slider and press **Delete sign**.

## Good to know

- Shots belong to the time, not to a passage record. Splitting or merging
  passages keeps them. If you trim a passage so that a shot falls outside it,
  the shot is hidden, and it returns if you undo the trim.
- Signs belong to the time too. Splitting or merging passages keeps them. A trim
  that cuts one out hides it until you undo the trim.
- With 3D off, shots only move and zoom the flat map.
- If your device is set to reduce motion, the camera cuts to each shot. In
  Follow and Orbit it cuts again if the boat nears the edge of the view. Signs
  appear without fading in.
