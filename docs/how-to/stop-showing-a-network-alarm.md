# Stop showing a network alarm

Use this for an alarm raised by another device on the boat that stays lit and
asks nothing of you, such as the plotter's Waypoint Passed. See [Stopping an
alarm you can't act on](../features/alarms.md#stopping-an-alarm-you-cant-act-on)
for what it does and which alarms can never be stopped.

## Stop showing one

1. Open the alarms drawer.
2. On the alarm's card, choose **Stop showing**. A card with no such button is a
   safety alarm and can't be stopped.
3. Read the confirmation, then choose **Stop showing** again.

The alarm leaves the board and the banner straight away, and stops arriving in
your notifications.

## Bring one back

1. Open **Settings → Alarms**.
2. Under **Ignored alarms**, find the alarm by its title. The date beside it is
   when you stopped showing it.
3. Choose **Restore**.

If the alarm is still active on the boat, it reappears and notifies you as a new
alarm.
