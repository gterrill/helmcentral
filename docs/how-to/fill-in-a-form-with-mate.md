# Fill in a form with Mate

See [Mate](../features/assistant.md#filling-in-forms) for what Mate fills in,
what it won't touch, and where it stops. These are the steps.

## Before the first form

1. Open **Settings → Vessel**.
2. Under **Owner & insurance**, enter the owner's name, phone and email, the
   insurer, the policy number, the home marina and berth, and the storm
   delegate.
3. Under **Particulars**, check the length overall and beam.
4. Choose **Save** in the bar across the top of the screen.

You can skip any of it. When a form needs something you haven't entered, Mate
asks you for it and offers to keep your answers.

## Fill in a form

1. Open Mate, either on the Mate page or in the Mate sheet.
2. Press the paperclip, or drop the PDF onto the message box, and type
   **Fill this in**.
3. If Mate needs something it doesn't have, it asks for all of it in one
   message. Answer in one reply.
4. If your answer belongs in the boat's record, Mate offers a card to save it.
   Press **Apply** to keep it for the next form.
5. When Mate has filled the form, a card appears under its reply. Press
   **Open the form** and read it through.

## Keep it or throw it away

- To keep it, check the **Title** and **Folder** on the card, then press
  **Save to Documents**. The card then links to the saved document.
- To save a copy on your device, press **Download**.
- To throw it away, press **Dismiss**. It was never in Documents, so there's
  nothing else to clean up.

If something is wrong, tell Mate in the same conversation and it fills the form
in again. Each attempt is its own card, so dismiss the ones you don't want.

## What you still do

Sign the form, and date it if you didn't ask Mate to. Mate leaves both blank on
purpose. Open the saved PDF, then print it or sign it however the insurer
wants, and send it.

## If Mate says it can't

- **No tick boxes found.** Mate fills in the lines it can and asks you which
  boxes to tick. If it can't tick them, tick them yourself on the downloaded
  copy.
- **A value won't fit.** Mate names the blank. Either shorten the value or
  write it in by hand.
- **A character it can't write.** Swap it for a plain letter, such as e for é,
  and ask again.
- **The form is a scan.** Mate can't read it. Ask the sender for the original
  PDF, or fill it in by hand.
