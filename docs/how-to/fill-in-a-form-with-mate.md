# Fill in a form with Mate

See [Mate](../features/assistant.md#filling-in-forms) for what Mate fills in,
what it will not touch, and where it stops. These are the steps.

## Before the first form

1. Open **Settings → Vessel**.
2. Under **Owner & insurance**, enter the owner's name, phone and email, the
   insurer, the policy number, the home marina and berth, and the storm
   delegate.
3. Under **Particulars**, check the length overall and beam.
4. Choose **Save** in the bar across the top of the screen.

You can skip any of it. Mate asks for what is missing when a form needs it, and
offers to keep your answers.

## Fill in a form

1. Open Mate, on the Mate page or in the Mate sheet.
2. Press the paperclip, or drop the PDF onto the message box, and type
   **Fill this in**.
3. If Mate needs something it does not have, it asks for all of it in one
   message. Answer in one reply.
4. If you answered with something that belongs in the boat's record, Mate offers
   a card to save it. Press **Apply** to keep it for the next form.
5. When Mate has filled the form, a card appears under its reply. Press
   **Open the form** and read it through.

## Keep it or throw it away

- To keep it, check the **Title** and **Folder** on the card, then press
  **Save to Documents**. The card then links to the saved document.
- To save a copy on your device, press **Download**.
- To throw it away, press **Dismiss**. It was never in Documents, so nothing
  else needs cleaning up.

If something is wrong, tell Mate in the same conversation and it fills the
form again. Each attempt is its own card, so dismiss the ones you do not want.

## What you still do

Sign the form, and date it if you did not ask Mate to. Mate leaves both blank on
purpose. Open the saved PDF, print it or sign it however the insurer wants, and
send it.

## If Mate says it cannot

- **No tick boxes found.** Mate fills in the lines it can and asks you which
  boxes to tick. Tick them yourself on the downloaded copy if it cannot.
- **A value will not fit.** Mate names the blank. Shorten the value, or write it
  in by hand.
- **A character it cannot write.** Replace it with a plain letter, such as a
  without an accent, and ask again.
- **The form is a scan.** Mate cannot read it. Ask the sender for the original
  PDF, or fill it in by hand.
