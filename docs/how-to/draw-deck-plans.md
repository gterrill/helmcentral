# Draw your deck plans

See [Inventory](../features/inventory-tracking.md#deck-plans) for what a deck
plan shows and where it appears. These are the steps for making one.

## What you need

A picture of the deck as a JPEG or PNG. The builder's general arrangement
drawing is ideal. If you have it as a PDF, export the page to an image first.
A clear photo of a printed drawing also works if it is taken square on.

## Add a deck and its picture

1. Open **Inventory**, then **Locations**, and open the menu at the top right.
   Choose **Decks**.
2. Press **New deck**, type a name such as `Main deck`, and press **Create**.
   The deck's page opens.
3. Under **Plan image**, press **Upload plan** and choose the picture. It
   appears at once under **Locations on this plan**.

To swap the picture later, press **Replace plan**. Outlines and pins stay where
they are on the image. If the new picture is a different shape, a warning tells
you to check them.

## Outline a location

1. On the deck's page, pick a location from the list beside the plan. A
   location that is already on another deck says which one. Outlining it here
   moves it to this deck.
2. Tap the plan at each corner of the location, going around it. Tap the first
   point again, or press **Finish outline**, to close the shape. You need at
   least three points.
3. Press **Save** in the bar at the top.

While you are drawing, **Backspace** takes back the last point, **Start over**
clears the shape, and **Escape** throws it away. Nothing reaches the boat's
records until you press **Save**.

## Reshape an outline

1. Pick the location in the list. Its corners show as round handles.
2. Drag a handle to move that corner. Tap a small handle between two corners to
   add a new corner there.
3. To remove a corner, tap it and press **Delete**. An outline always keeps at
   least three corners. The arrow keys nudge a selected corner, and holding
   **Shift** nudges further.
4. To take the location off the plan, press **Remove from plan**. Its bins lose
   their pins.
5. Press **Save**.

## Pin a bin

1. Pick the bin's location in the list, then pick the bin underneath it. A bin
   can only be pinned once its location is outlined.
2. Tap the plan where the bin sits. Tap somewhere else to move the pin.
3. To remove a pin, press **Remove pin**.
4. Press **Save**.

## Rename or delete a deck

Rename a deck by editing its name on the deck's page and pressing **Save**. To
delete it, use the menu at the top right of the deck's page and confirm. The
confirmation says how many locations lose their outline. The locations and bins
stay, and the picture stays in Documents.

## Look at the plan

Open **Locations** and press **Plan** at the top. Pick a deck tab, then tap a
location's outline to open it or a pin to open that bin. A location's page and
a bin's page show the same plan with their place marked.
