# Photograph your gear

[Inventory](../features/inventory-tracking.md#tags-and-photos) explains what
photos are for. These are the steps for adding them, either to an existing
item or on the spot while adding a new one from a bin.

## Add photos to an item

1. Open the item from Equipment, or tap its bin and press **Open** under
   its photo.
2. In the item's Photos section, press **Take photo** to use the camera
   directly, or **Add from library** to pick one or more already on the
   phone.
3. Each photo appears as a thumbnail once it uploads. Press **Make cover**
   on the one that should show first everywhere the item is listed, and
   **Remove** on any you don't want kept.

Every photo is downscaled before it uploads, so this stays quick even over a
Tailscale connection with several photos to send.

If a picture is identical to a file already in Documents, it is linked to the
item rather than stored a second time. It shows up in both the Photos row and
the item's Documents list.

## Add a new item with photos from a bin

This is the quickest way to record what's in a bin while you're standing at the
open locker:

1. Open the bin's page.
2. Press **Take photo** as many times as you need. A front and a back of the
   same packet is normal.
3. Type a name and, if there's more than one, a quantity.
4. Press **Save**. The form clears and the camera is ready for the next thing
   in the bin.

If a photo fails to upload (a dropped connection is the usual cause), the item
still saves under its name. A **Retry** button appears next to a note saying
how many photos didn't make it. Press it once you're back in range. It
re-sends only the ones that failed.

## Remove or replace a photo

Press **Remove** on its thumbnail in the item's own Photos row. This takes the
photo off this item. The picture stays in Documents, since the same picture
can be filed against more than one item.

If no other item uses the picture and it isn't filed in Documents, a second
option, **Remove and delete**, appears next to Remove. Use it when you want
the picture gone for good, such as a blurry shot you're replacing.

Deleting the whole item works the same way. When the item has photos that fit
that description, the confirm dialog offers a checkbox, "Also delete N
photo(s) only this item uses," and shows a small thumbnail of each one once
you tick it, so you can see what's about to go. Leave it unchecked to keep
those pictures in Documents after the item is gone. Check it to remove them
with the item.
