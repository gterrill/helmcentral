# Keep what a Mate conversation worked out

See [Mate](../features/assistant.md#keeping-what-a-conversation-worked-out) for
what the summary contains and why it exists. These are the steps.

## Save a summary

1. Open the conversation in Mate, on the Mate page or in the Mate sheet. The
   button is available once Mate has answered at least once and is not in the
   middle of an answer.
2. Press **Summarise to note**, the page icon beside the microphone. A review
   window opens and says **Writing the summary** for a few seconds.
3. Read the draft. Change the title if you want, and edit the text as you would
   any note.
4. Under **Link to equipment**, leave ticked the equipment the note is about
   and untick any that is wrong.
5. Press **Save**. A message confirms it is in Notes, with an **Open note**
   link.

Press **Cancel** to leave without saving anything.

## Update the note later

Keep talking in the same conversation, then press **Update note** where
**Summarise to note** was. The review window shows a fresh summary of the whole
conversation. Equipment already linked is ticked; unticking it removes the
link. Press **Update note** to replace the note's title and text. No second note
is made.

## If it says there is nothing to keep

Mate writes nothing when the conversation has settled nothing about your boat
yet. Carry on the conversation, then try again. If the window shows any other
message, it is the reason the summary could not be written or saved, such as
Mate being switched off or the link to the model being down. Press **Cancel**
and try again once that is fixed.
