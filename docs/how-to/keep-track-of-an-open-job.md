# Keep track of an open job

Write down a problem you've found and haven't fixed yet, then close it when
it's done. For how open jobs differ from rules, see
[Maintenance](../features/maintenance.md).

## Add a job

1. Open **Inventory → Maintenance**, or the page of the item the problem is on.
2. In the **Open jobs** group, tap **Add job**. On an item's page, the job
   starts on that item.
3. Type a title, such as "Leaks at the outlet".
4. Choose the item if it isn't already set. Leave it as **No item** for
   something that isn't one piece of gear.
5. If you want it fixed by a certain day, set **Before**. Add notes if they'll
   help whoever fixes it.
6. Tap **Save**. To add photos, use the photo row that appears, then tap
   **Close**.

You can also tell Mate: "add the shower pump to the jobs list". Tap **Apply**
on the card it gives you.

## Change a job

Tap the job's title to change its item, title, notes, Before date or photos.

## Mark a job done

1. Tap **Done** on the job.
2. Check the date, which starts as today, and the kind. It starts as **Repair**.
3. For a repair, check **Started showing**. It starts as the day you opened the
   job. Change it to the day the fault really began, or clear it if the fault didn't show then. On an engine,
   or on the house battery or anything that charges it, its readings from
   then until the repair are left out of what Helmcentral learns as normal.
4. Type the hours off the gauge if the job is on an engine or another piece of
   gear with an hour meter. Add what you did, who did it, the cost and any parts.
5. Tap **Mark done**. One line goes into the service log and the job closes. Its
   photos go onto that line.

If you leave the notes blank, the line reads as the job's title.

## Drop a job

1. Tap **Drop** on the job.
2. Say why if you like, such as "fixed itself after a tighten".
3. Tap **Drop job**. Nothing is written to the service log.

## Look at closed jobs

Tap **Show closed** in the Open jobs group. Each closed job shows whether it was
done or dropped, the date, and the reason if you gave one. A job that was done
is in the service log under the item.
